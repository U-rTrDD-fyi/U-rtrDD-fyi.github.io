Description

TopRow puts a number row on top of the stock iOS keyboard, so you stop switching to 123 for every digit. New in 2.0: change any other key on it too, and save and share your setups.

  • Letters: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 0
  • Shift / Caps Lock: ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( )
  • 123 and #+=: an extra ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) row under the digits

Every key, your way (new in 2.0):

  • Retype, add, remove or reorder the keys on the letters, Shift, 123 and #+= pages, up to 12 per row: put ñ next to L, , . next to M, or _ ~ + = [ ] on the 123 page
  • Shift rows follow your letters in capitals, and keys you keep from Apple's row keep their long-press accents and your region's currency signs
  • Rename the 123, ABC and #+= keys

Setups (new in 2.0): save your settings under a name, switch between them, and share them with friends as a file or as text to paste. TopRow also saves a backup before every reset, so you can undo it.

Everything is adjustable, and it all applies live (no respring):

  • Edit the top row for each keyboard page: 1 to 10 keys, up to 8 characters per key (emoji and .com work too)
  • Lowercase Row When Auto-Capitalized: keep the digits at the start of a sentence; symbols only with Shift or Caps Lock
  • Symbol Hints: each number key shows its Shift symbol in the corner
  • Key Height (80–130%) and Bottom Area (60–130%) sliders, with exact-number entry
  • Landscape on or off, with optional separate landscape rows
  • Turn TopRow off per app, or for email, web and Twitter fields
  • Preview fields right in Settings

Works where you type: apps, Safari, password fields, share sheets, and password managers' AutoFill sheets. Number, phone and passcode pads are left alone, and the Bottom Area slider plays nicely with dock bars from other tweaks.

Compatibility: iPhone on rootless jailbreaks, iOS 15 and later. Developed and tested on iOS 17.3.1 with Dopamine; reports from other versions are welcome. The Keys page changes the US English (QWERTY) layout; other languages keep their own keys. Does nothing on iPad, whose keyboards already put digits on their keys. Uninstall any other number-row tweak before installing: two of them on the same keyboard means overlapping rows and layout bugs.

Free and open source under the MIT license: github.com/aayy-dev/TopRow. If something breaks, use Share Diagnostics in TopRow's settings and attach the file to an issue.

Changelog

2.0.0

  • New Keys page: change any key on the letters, Shift / Caps Lock, 123 and #+= pages. Add, remove, reorder or retype keys, up to 12 per row. Shift rows follow your letters in capitals unless you set them. Keys kept from Apple's row keep their long-press accents and your region's currency signs. Applies to the US English (QWERTY) layout in every size and keyboard type, and in landscape even with Show in Landscape off; other languages and the email and web-address number pages keep their own keys. The page has its own Preview field.
  • New Setups page: save your settings under a name (up to 50) and switch between them. Share a setup as a text file or as text to paste; friends import it from the clipboard or Files, see what's in it, and apply it when they choose. Excluded Apps stay out of shared setups unless you include them.
  • Automatic backups: TopRow saves your settings before Reset to Defaults, Reset Keys or applying a setup, and keeps the 3 newest, so each of those can be undone.
  • Settings pages refresh when TopRow's settings change in another app (Settings or TweakSettings).
  • Rename the #+= key too. The 123 and ABC labels moved to the Keys page.
  • Settings: Keys sits right under Enabled, with a NEW badge until you open it, and the Extra Row section is now called Top Row.
  • Fix: in landscape on some iPhones (the 15 Pro among them), an extra row with fewer than 10 keys was stretched past the right edge, hiding its last keys.
  • Fix: NewTerm froze on launch at some keyboard heights, including the default Key Height on some iPhones.

1.0.1

  • Fix apps (Settings, Sileo and others) hanging on launch on some setups: TopRow no longer calls UIKit while the app is still loading.
  • CarPlay keyboards stay stock. The car screen keeps its keyboard height, so the extra row covered the letters.
  • Share Diagnostics now also includes TopRow's log from every app and the last 3 days of iOS crash and hang reports, with the stack of any thread TopRow is on.
