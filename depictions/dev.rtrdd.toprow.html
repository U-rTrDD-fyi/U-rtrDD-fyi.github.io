Description

TopRow puts a number row on top of the stock iOS keyboard, so you stop switching to 123 for every digit.

  • Letters: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 0
  • Shift / Caps Lock: ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( )
  • 123 and #+=: an extra ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) row under the digits

Everything is adjustable, and it all applies live (no respring):

  • Edit every row: 1 to 10 keys each, up to 8 characters per key (emoji and .com work too)
  • Lowercase Row When Auto-Capitalized: keep the digits at the start of a sentence; symbols only with Shift or Caps Lock
  • Symbol Hints: each number key shows its Shift symbol in the corner
  • Key Height (80–130%) and Bottom Area (60–130%) sliders, with exact-number entry
  • Landscape on or off, with optional separate landscape rows
  • Rename the 123 and ABC keys
  • Turn the row off per app, or for email, web and Twitter fields
  • Preview field right in Settings

Works where you type: apps, Safari, password fields, share sheets, and password managers' AutoFill sheets. Number, phone and passcode pads are left alone, and the Bottom Area slider plays nicely with dock bars from other tweaks.

Compatibility: iPhone on rootless jailbreaks, iOS 15 and later. Developed and tested on iOS 17.3.1 with Dopamine; reports from other versions are welcome. Does nothing on iPad, whose keyboards already put digits on their keys. Uninstall any other number-row tweak before installing: two of them on the same keyboard means overlapping rows and layout bugs.

Free and open source under the MIT license: github.com/U-rTrDD-fyi/TopRow. If something breaks, use Share Diagnostics in TopRow's settings and attach the file to an issue.
